# References for experimentally dual amyloid / antimicrobial peptides

Primary and review sources used to assemble
`dual_amyAMP_experimental.fasta` / metadata. Prefer DOI; PMID given when common.

## Reviews (best entry points)

1. **Tang Y, Zhang D, Zhang Y, Chen Y, Liu F, Ren Y, et al.** Exploring pathological link between antimicrobial and amyloid peptides. *Chem Soc Rev.* 2024;53(17):8713–8763.  
   DOI: [10.1039/D3CS00848A](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3CS00848A)  
   — Comprehensive AMP↔amyloid link; lists dual-function examples (PG-1, plantaricin A, uperin 3.5, magainin, dermaseptin S9, Aβ, hIAPP, α-syn, SAA, …).

2. **Landau M et al.** Structural Mimicry in Microbial and Antimicrobial Amyloids. *Annu Rev Biochem.* 2022.  
   DOI: [10.1146/annurev-biochem-032620-105157](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-biochem-032620-105157)

## Disease amyloids with antimicrobial activity

3. **Soscia SJ, et al.** The Alzheimer's Disease-Associated Amyloid β-Protein Is an Antimicrobial Peptide. *PLoS One.* 2010;5:e9505.  
   PMID: [20209079](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20209079/) · DOI: [10.1371/journal.pone.0009505](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0009505)

4. **Kumar DKV, et al.** Amyloid-β peptide protects against microbial infection in mouse and worm models of Alzheimer’s disease. *Sci Transl Med.* 2016;8:340ra72.  
   PMID: [26822600](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26822600/) · DOI: [10.1126/scitranslmed.aaf1059](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aaf1059)

5. **Park S-C, et al.** Functional characterization of amyloid-β antimicrobial peptide (review of subsequent literature). See also Spitzer et al., *Sci Rep.* 2016;6:32228 (Aβ variants agglutinate microbes).

6. **α-Synuclein antimicrobial activity** — e.g. Park et al. / related PD-innate immunity studies (PMID 27196974, 28874589).

7. **hIAPP / SAA antimicrobial reports** — summarized in Tang *et al.* 2024 (refs therein); SAA AMP literature includes PMID 28743747.
